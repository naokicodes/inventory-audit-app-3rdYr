# ADR-017 Commi access, not a sub-commi

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-28
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: the 10-28 item 73 (a standing commi membership for station cooks)

## Context

The griller at Likod and some kitchen cooks back up raw meat themselves. A "sub-commi" per
station was considered.

## Decision

- **One commissary; more people may work it.** All raw meat lands on commi stock first. A cook
  who backs up meat asks the commi for it, then records the yield and the shipment on the
  commissary page.
- **At Silingan**: whoever the roster places on Commissary has access while there; other commi
  work (for example backing up shortplate for braised beef / nilaga) is a head-chef pick for
  the day.
- **At Likod**: all grillers have standing commi access (they all prep).
- Skewering and portioning (tuhog) is commi work wherever it physically happens.
- Commi meat requests are internal requests.

## Why

One stock truth for raw meat, and no second commi to reconcile.

## Consequences

Shipment destinations gain Likod (A12).

## Reopen only if

A site gets its own commissary (for example a future branch).
