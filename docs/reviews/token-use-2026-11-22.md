"Sent in all" adds every call's input: new tokens, cache writes and cache reads. The pr-reviewer
subagent's own tokens are in separate logs and are not counted here (it runs on Sonnet).

## What the numbers say

1. **Every session starts at about 55k tokens** before any work: Claude Code's own prompt and
   tools, CLAUDE.md, the command file, and whatever mods, plugins and MCP servers are loaded. The
   split is not in the logs; `/context` shows it.
2. **A session costs its number of calls times its size.** A build with a click-through makes three
   to four times the calls of a small step at about twice the average size: five to ten times the
   tokens.
3. **The model's own writing is under 1%** of what is sent (ui-shell: 53k of 11.2M). Writing less
   code, or a lower effort, changes little here; a shorter or smaller session changes a lot.
4. **What enters the session stays for every later call.** The three big steps made 20 to 57
   browser calls each for their click-throughs; `docs/rules-for-claude-code.md` (about 8k tokens) was
   read whole twice; `public/kitchen-sales.html` was read six times across the day.
5. **graphify** was called twice in 17 sessions (one `graphify query`, one `graphify update`): the
   specs are read with awk and the code with grep. Its cost is its few CLAUDE.md lines.
