# ADR-025 Bar: exact sales check, not blind

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-25
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Drinks sold as they are and dessert slices can be checked exactly against Loyverse.

## Decision

- Bottles, cans and dessert slices get expected vs counted from Loyverse sales, like meat;
  desserts first.
- Bar counts are not blind.
- Raw bar items stay side-sheet style for now.

## Why

An exact check is cheap where items are sold whole.

## Consequences

Sides family (A8).

## Reopen only if

The bar's raw items need the same check.
