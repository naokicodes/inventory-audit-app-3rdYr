# ADR-031 Pizza: dough first (partly decided)

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-25
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Pizza is a station shared by Silingan and Likod, rung on both accounts.

## Decision

- Dough matters most: counted in balls, made in containers of 12 with a set flour amount; new
  dough is entered in steps of 12. The dough row works like meat (begin + made - cooked -
  in-house = end). Old dough (French loaf, pita) and boxes sent to FC are adjustments.
- Two bases; a mozzarella standard per pizza type and per station.
- Shared pantry items are counted on both sheets and summed.
- Pizza is the last side sheet; first a skeleton that shows the pizzaman the pizzas sold.

## Why

Dough is where the losses are.

## Consequences

Open (A11): whose stock the shared station's is; which pantry it draws from.

## Reopen only if

The station stops being shared.
