# Pantry

Status: IRL Confirmed (Silingan, FC) - FLOW Drafted - APP Decided

## IRL - how it works today
Sheet "Inventory Pantry" (Silingan) / "Inventory Form Kitchen - PANTRY" (FC)
(source: photos 2026-10-24).
- Columns: ITEMS, BEG, NEW STOCK, WASTE, END, PO (REM, QTY), REMARKS. FC's
  template prints END twice (a template slip). Waste goes on a separate paper.
- Silingan about 45 items, FC about 45: seasonings and sauces, cubes, noodles
  and pasta, oil, sugars, salt, flours, mixes, flakes, canned goods, and some
  supplies (skewers, gloves at FC).
- Units: gal, pack, pcs, tube, bottle, can, container, box, kg, tie; fractions
  down to eighths ("1 1/8 pack", "3/4 container"); "OS" = out of stock.
- PO remarks carry urgency and brand ("ASAP, add tomorrow").
- Counted exactly (unlike veggies). Podium keeps a logbook for pantry arrivals.
- Pizza stations keep small working stocks of some pantry items (pizza.md).

## FLOW - how they will use the app (drafted)
Same as veggies: the assigned counter enters END per item in any unit, PO
quantity per item, remarks; the landing person consolidates the PO.
