# ADR-029 Management view: Spent and Used

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-24
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Money and stock flow differently.

## Decision

- Show **Spent** (purchases paid) and **Used** (stock consumed or lost) separately, clearly
  labelled.
- Per day, week and month, checked at night. Supervisors see it too.

## Why

Mixing the two misleads.

## Consequences

Management view (area 17).

## Reopen only if

Management asks for a single combined figure.
