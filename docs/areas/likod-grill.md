# Likod grill landing

Status: IRL Confirmed - FLOW None - APP None

## IRL - how it works today
Sheet "Likod ni Silingan - Landing" kept by the griller (source: photos
2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24). Likod is a separate restaurant; its only core sheet
now is the grill's (its own kitchen comes later).
- **Grill items** (BEG, NEW S., POS, INHOUSE, OVR/SHRT, REMARKS, END): skewers
  and grill portions (pork, chicken, tuna skewers, belly strips, tahong,
  pompano, french chops, pork steak, rib-atayaki, sisig, tuna kinilaw). Mixed
  units in one row (g, kg, pcs).
- **Raw meats** (BEG, NEW S., TUHOG / PORTION, REMARKS, END): whole chicken,
  pork belly, tahong, pompano, french cut, pork steak, ribs, tuna, jowl.
  Skewering and portioning is done at the commi, which prepares the final
  products. Side notes move portions between items ("2 portions to porkito").
- What arrives matches the commi sheet's Likod column (10-24: jowl 4.4 kg,
  tuna 2 packs, chicken 5), so the app's shipment + acknowledge fits.
- **PO blocks:** grill (egg, onions, cabbage, charcoal, banana leaves),
  bar (milk, fruit, syrups), others (gloves, tissue).
- POS = Likod Loyverse sales of the specials menu. Skewer dishes have 6 sticks:
  Cue Medley = 2 porkito + 2 yaki to eh (chicken) + 2 tuna skewers; Squeal and
  Cheep = 3 porkito + 3 yaki to eh.
- How Likod connects (confirmed 2026-10-25): commi "Likod out" -> grill
  raw-meat arrivals; specials menu -> grill items; coffee and cakes -> Likod
  bar sheet; drinks -> bottles & cans sheet; pizza -> the shared station;
  Silingan-menu dishes sold at Likod -> Silingan's kitchen.
- Likod's own kitchen opens roughly before the new year.
