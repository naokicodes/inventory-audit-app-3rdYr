# ADR-065 FC: kitchen sheets in the app, the rest on paper

- Status: Accepted 2026-11-14
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: for FC only, the 2026-09-28 side sheets for Pizza and Scullery (B2) and
  fc-scullery's "decided as a side sheet"

## Context

FC is small and across the city. Its paper sheets: landing, veggies, pantry, bar and dining,
pizza, scullery and a cashier list.

## Decision

- In the app at FC: the meat landing, Veggies, Pantry, and a Bar sheet for drinks sold as they
  are: Pepsi (litro, panalo, sulit), Mountain Dew (litro, panalo), Sprite sulit, Coke swakto,
  Mismo, Milkis, Gatorade 500 ml, mineral water (1 L, 500 ml). Nothing more.
- On paper: scullery, the cashier list, dining supplies, bar raws and pizza. Their sheets exist
  switched off, so one can be turned on later.
- FC's pizzaman reads pizza sales on Kitchen Sales (FC side) and writes them on paper (ADR-066).

## Why

What needs watching at FC is the kitchen.

## Consequences

FC's drinks get the bar's exact check (ADR-025) once FC's sales are in (ADR-066). The boodle deal
includes four free small soft drinks; when those run out, customers may upgrade all four to panalo
("UPGRADE PANALO"). bar-check maps both. 7UP is gone (counted as Mountain Dew).

## Reopen only if

FC's paper sheets show losses worth tracking.
