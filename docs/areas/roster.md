# Roster

Status: IRL Written - FLOW Drafted - APP Decided

## IRL - how it works today
- Supervisors post the day's roster in each restaurant's group chat: date and
  call time, then each station with its people. Silingan's lists supervisors,
  cashier, head chef / dispatch, expo / cold, kitchen, kitchen scullery,
  pizza / bread, bar, dining (opener / mid / closer with breaks), function,
  podium, food server, commissary, buss, scullery, uniform, rest days, people
  lent to Likod, totals (present, day off, floater, head count). Likod's lists
  pizza chef, griller, dispatch, barista / cashier, waiters, podium, buss, day off.
- A person's role is tied to their station. When someone is off, the post says
  who covers their station.
- Silingan and Likod change often (absences, sudden quits, medical); FC keeps
  its weekly roster.
- Supervisors make the weekly roster on the weekend, in advance; the CEO checks
  and approves it. Any supervisor can make daily changes.
- Sources: roster message pasted 2026-10-22; chat 2026-10-24.

## FLOW - how they will use the app (drafted)
1. Weekend: a supervisor builds next week from the weekly template.
2. The CEO approves it.
3. Each morning (or as needed) a supervisor makes daily changes: day off,
   cover, someone lent to the other restaurant.
4. Tonight's roster drives the login list and the automatic sheet assignments.

## APP
- Decided: separate staff table, optional link to a login account (10-21);
  weekly template + daily changes (11); stored per day: site, station, person,
  on duty / rest day; head count feeds the meal budget; no shift times or
  time-in/out (23); management is read-only except approving the weekly roster
  (23); a specific supervisor can be blocked from editing (23).
- Not specced. Queue item A6.

- Decided (handoff 55): a person lent to another restaurant for the day (any of
  the three, FC included) appears on both rosters - "lent out" at home,
  "working" where they go - and counts in the head count only where they work.
