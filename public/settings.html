Conversion Standards

The conversion-efficiency rate for one (commissary meat, restaurant meat) pair - e.g. "Jowl → FC's Bagnet: 0.3 units per kg." Used by the Shipments form to compute an implied-input hint as lines are typed. Not the same as a Shipment Preset (that's a demand mix, can have several); a Standard is a rate fact, exactly one per pairing.
