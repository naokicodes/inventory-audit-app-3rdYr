# Pizza stations

Status: IRL Confirmed - FLOW None - APP Partly decided (dough in containers of 12)

## IRL - how it works today
Two stations (source: photos 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24).

**Silingan / Likod shared station** ("Inventory Pizza"), at Likod, with one
pizzaman from each restaurant; both names go on the sheet.
- Columns: ITEMS, BEG, NEW STOCK, COOKED, INHOUSE, WASTE, END, ACTUAL, PO QTY.
- About 27 items: bread flour, yeast, sugars, glutinous flour, butter cream,
  milk, olives, pineapple, ham, pepperoni, sauces (and take-out sachets), tie
  box, basil, tomato sauce and paste, pizza box, cream cheese, glassine,
  mozzarella, parmesan, liquid seasoning, prosciutto, REMAINING DOUGH.
- Notes at the bottom: pizza boxes sent to FC; old dough turned into French
  loaf or pita.

**FC station** ("Inventory Form Kitchen - PIZZA", one pizzaman). Same columns
plus NOTES. About 27 items, including bread improver, gluten flour, ground
beef, pesto oil, firewood (kahoy) and the same REMAINING DOUGH row.

Both:
- Most rows are begin / new / end like a side sheet. Units: kg, sack, packs,
  fractions, bottle, can, box, tie.
- **Dough:** made in containers of 12; new dough only comes in steps of 12.
  It works like meat: begin + made - cooked - in-house = end
  (Silingan 10-23: 28 + 72 - 58 - 2 = 40; 10-24: 40 + 48 - 53 = 35;
  FC 10-24: 9 + 36 made).
- COOKED / INHOUSE / WASTE are filled for dough only; waste goes on a separate
  paper.
- Flour used vs dough made gives a natural standard (about 220-250 g of flour
  per ball on the two Silingan days).
- Pizza is rung on both restaurants' Loyverse accounts (like the Silingan
  kitchen for now); at the shared station both take from the same dough and
  mozzarella. Dough is what matters most.
- Two bases: mozzarella and cream cheese. Each pizza type uses one; the
  mozzarella standard per pizza differs between the shared station and FC.

## APP
- Decided (handoff 37, 51):
  - Dough is counted in balls; making dough is logged as containers of 12,
    with a set amount of flour per container (gives the flour check).
  - Dough and mozzarella get a set amount per pizza and a variance, like meat;
    mozzarella standard per pizza type, per station.
  - Old dough (French loaf, pita) and boxes to FC are adjustments
    (conversion, transfer), like meat.
  - Items shared with the pantry are counted on both sheets and summed.
  - Pizza is the last side sheet to build. First, a skeleton: the pizzaman
    sees pizzas sold (from Loyverse) on their sheet during testing.
