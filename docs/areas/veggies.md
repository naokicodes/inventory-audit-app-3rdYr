# Kitchen veggies

Status: IRL Confirmed (Silingan, FC) - FLOW Drafted - APP Decided

## IRL - how it works today
Sheet "Inventory Form Kitchen - VEG/SPICES" at Silingan and FC (source: photos
2026-10-24).
- Header: name, date, PO. Columns: ITEMS, BEG, NEW STOCKS, FOR / POR, COOKED,
  INHOUSE, WASTE, END, REMARKS, PO (REM, QTY).
- Staff fill BEG, NEW STOCKS, END, REMARKS and PO. COOKED, INHOUSE and WASTE
  are meant to be filled from other records (FC sometimes writes INHOUSE);
  waste is written on a separate paper. In the app these columns are shown,
  computed from their own entries.
- Silingan: about 33 items (onion, garlic, potato, carrots, cabbage, beans,
  batong, talong, ampalaya, kalabasa, atsal, okra, cucumber, kolikot, jalapeno,
  ginger, cauliflower, broccoli, lemons, sayote, tanglad, bokchoy, parsley,
  kamatis, lemonsito, pako, mushroom, egg, radish, lakatan, celery, pechay,
  leeks).
- FC: about 31 items, a similar list plus kangkong, upo, arugula, dahon sili,
  sibuyas dahon, quail egg, peanuts, taro leaves, green peas, buns.
- Units are mixed, even within one row: kg, g, pcs, half pieces, tray, stalk,
  pack, tie, tub, sipi (a hand of bananas), and peso value at Silingan for some
  herbs (tanglad; FC counts it in grams). "OS" or "-" = out of stock.
- Likod has no veggie sheet yet; its kitchen opens roughly before the new year.
  Its sheet starts as a copy of Silingan's list and stays closed until then.
- Veggies are eyeballed at count time. On arrival the kitchen only confirms
  the veggies are there; the buyer already weighed them.

## FLOW - how they will use the app (drafted)
1. The assigned kitchen counter opens Veggies (separate from Pantry; one person
   may hold both tonight).
2. For each item: END in any unit, a one-tap "out of stock", optional remark.
3. PO quantity per item; the landing person consolidates and may cut it.

## APP
- Decided: separate sheets, separately assignable (16); flexible units with
  conversions, any-unit entry, fractions (17); usage = begin + received - end;
  COOKED / INHOUSE / WASTE shown from their own entries (handoff 38).
- From the paper: "peso value" as a unit; one-tap "out of stock".
