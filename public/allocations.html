Allocations

One log for every non-sale reduction (or transfer) to a restaurant's meat stock - wastage, staff meals, spoilage, damaged goods, and location-to-location transfers. Landing's Adjustments cell is a read-only sum of this log. Each entry here is its own row - two wastage entries the same day both count, rather than one overwriting the other.

This type needs both a from and a to location - manage the list of locations in Settings.

This converts stock of the "Meat" field above into a different item - "Quantity" above is how much leaves that item, "Converted qty" is how much the other item gains. They don't need to match 1:1.
