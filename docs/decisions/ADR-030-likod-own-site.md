# ADR-030 Likod is its own site

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-25
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Likod shares a commi with Silingan and sells some Silingan dishes.

## Decision

- Likod has its own arrivals, POs, cashier and landing. Its core sheet now is the grill's.
- Likod sales of Silingan-menu dishes count against Silingan's kitchen.
- Mapping: commi "Likod out" -> grill raw arrivals; specials -> grill items; coffee and cakes ->
  Likod bar; drinks -> bottles & cans; pizza -> the shared station; Silingan dishes sold at
  Likod -> Silingan.
- Likod gets Veggies and Pantry sheets, closed until its kitchen opens (roughly before the new
  year). Likod cooks its own staff meals.

## Why

It matches how Likod runs.

## Consequences

Likod family (A12). Recipe facts such as the six-stick skewer dishes are settings data.

## Reopen only if

Likod merges operationally with Silingan.
