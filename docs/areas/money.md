# Money and operating expenses

Status: IRL Told - FLOW None - APP None

## IRL
- Two flows that meet at buying (money out, stock in) and using (stock out,
  valued in pesos). Revenue stays in Loyverse / SPOS (D3).
- In-house and cash-advance slips are settled at the weekly payroll.
- Buying money (Naoki, 2026-11-14): the COO gives the buyers a budget; the buyers photograph
  their receipts and return the change; after service the cashier pays the COO back the day's
  total from the takings. The cashier also pays some purchases from the till. The cashiers' tally
  sheet records the day's money in and out. None of this is stored in this app (ADR-059,
  ADR-068).
