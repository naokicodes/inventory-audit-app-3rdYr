# ADR-016 One unit per meat on the landing

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-28
- Decided by: Naoki
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Whether one meat could be counted by pieces AND by weight on the landing was open since 10-24.

## Decision

- Each meat has **one unit on the landing**.
- A kg figure at a landing means commi work, recorded on the commissary page (ADR-017).
- Commissary shipments are written per destination as kg and as portions.
- Side and station sheets keep flexible units (ADR-024); dough is counted in balls (ADR-031).

## Why

The meat math, and yield (output always kg, Path 1), rely on one unit.

## Consequences

Closes the "pieces AND weight" question.

## Reopen only if

A meat cannot be counted sensibly in one unit.
