Terminal

A type-first alternative to the Commissary Shipments form, for people who'd rather type than click. Discord-slash-command style: one input line, with a hint bar and a filtering dropdown that follow whichever slot the cursor is currently in, plus a persistent guide above it showing the whole command's shape at once - filled slots in green with their resolved value, the active slot highlighted, upcoming ones dimmed, and a bad token flagged red right where it is, so you don't lose track of what you already typed once you've moved past it. Once a commissary meat and restaurant are both picked, destination lines already covered by a saved preset for that pair show their default quantity and sort to the top of the list - still just autofill, edit or ignore it freely. Enter on a complete line calls the same POST /api/commissary/shipments endpoint the GUI form uses - this is a real write, not a preview.
